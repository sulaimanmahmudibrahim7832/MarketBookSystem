Product Inventory & Tracking Management System (PITMS)

System summary

This prototype provides a simple interface to manage business inventory and product movement. Use the Dashboard to get a snapshot of active products, current inventory and recent activity. The Account page stores business information and status. Products, Import and Export pages are used to manage product records and record incoming/outgoing stock. Tracking and History help analyze movements and review past transactions.
